# **🐺 Fightcade Plus (Cerberus)**

[*🇧🇷 Leia em Português (Read in Portuguese)*](README_br.md)  
**Developed with a focus on high performance and optimization for all PCs.**  
**Fightcade Plus** is an enhancement script (mod) for the official Fightcade client. It injects quality-of-life (QoL) features, advanced geographic filters, and a community reputation system—all without causing any slowdowns or FPS drops during your matches.

## **✨ Features**

1. **Auto Join Channels:** Fightcade will automatically join your main game room upon opening.  
2. **Geographic Country Filter:** Hide players from distant regions with a single click. Highly resilient to offline players and fully reactive.  
3. **Chat Visual Improvements:** See the exact Ping (in numbers), Country Flags, Player Ranks, and Status (Online/Away) directly inside the room's chat.  
4. **Global Reputation System (👍/👎):** Highlight players with good connections or permanently hide toxic/laggy players from the entire Fightcade interface.  
5. **Privacy Mode (Blur):** Apply a blur effect to specific messages or the entire chat window to avoid distractions while playing or streaming.  
6. **Premium Themes Unlock:** Get immediate access to exclusive color palettes and interface themes (such as the *Bretema* dark mode).

## **🚀 How to Install (Simplified Version)**

To make things easier, we provide a ready-to-use version of Fightcade (Plug & Play), with all Cerberus optimizations already applied out of the box.

1. Go to the [**Releases (Latest Version)**](https://github.com/Cerberus-BR/FightcadePlus/releases/latest) tab.  
2. Download the compressed file (.zip or .rar) listed under *Assets*.  
3. Extract the folder anywhere on your computer.  
4. Open the Fightcade executable normally and have fun\!

### **Manual Installation (For Advanced Users)**

If you know what you are doing and prefer to inject the script into your own clean Fightcade installation, simply download the cerberus.js file from this repository and use it to replace the original script inside your emulator's plugin folder.

## **⚙️ How to Use**

Once inside any game room (e.g., KOF 2002, Street Fighter III), look at the top right corner of the Chat window, right next to the online players counter.  
You will spot a gear icon (⚙️).  
Click on it to open the **Cerberus Panel**. From there, you can:

* Change the interface language (English, Portuguese, Spanish).  
* Toggle any visual modifications on or off.  
* Configure which countries are allowed to show up in the filter.

To use the **Reputation System**, simply hover your mouse over a player's name in the sidebar list or chat. A floating menu will appear, allowing you to Rate (👍), Negative (👎), or Clear (🧹) the player's status.
